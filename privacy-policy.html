Privacy Policy

App: Awqat – Prayer Times  |  Package: com.muslimapp.awqat
Last updated: May 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Awqat – Prayer Times ("the App") handles your information. We are committed to protecting your privacy.

1. Information We Collect

Location data: The App requests access to your device's location (GPS) solely to calculate accurate Islamic prayer times and Qibla direction for your area. Location data is used in real time and is never stored, transmitted, or shared with any third party.

No account required: The App does not require you to create an account, register, or provide any personal information such as your name or email address.

2. Information We Do Not Collect

  • We do not collect or store your name, email, phone number, or any personal identifiers.
  • We do not track your precise location over time.
  • We do not use advertising networks or analytics SDKs.
  • We do not sell or share your data with third parties.

3. Internet Access & Firebase

The App connects to the internet to fetch daily Islamic content (posts, reminders) from a Firebase Firestore database. This connection does not transmit any personal data — only a request for publicly available content is made.

4. Notifications

The App may send local push notifications to remind you of prayer times. These notifications are generated entirely on your device and do not involve sending your data to any server.

5. Microphone

The App may request microphone access for the voice Dhikr counter feature. Audio is processed locally on your device in real time and is never recorded or transmitted.

6. Storage / Media Access

The App may request access to your device's media storage to allow administrators to upload timetable images. This access is only used when you explicitly choose a file and is not used to access any other files.

7. Data Security

Since we do not collect or store personal data, there is no personal data to secure. All communication with Firebase uses encrypted HTTPS connections.

8. Children's Privacy

The App is suitable for all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date. Continued use of the App after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
